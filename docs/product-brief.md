# Teraz — product brief

**Problem:** Customers buying ties and belts struggle to pick pieces that match their suit, shirt, shoes and the occasion.

**Primary user:** Men shopping for business and occasion accessories (weddings, interviews, formal events), on their phone.

## Core features
1. Browse and search the Teraz tie and belt catalogue
2. Personal styling: enter suit, shirt and shoe colours and the occasion to get matching ties and belts
3. Favourites, cart, checkout and order history

**Device feature:** Camera — capture a photo of the outfit (Phase 2)

**External API:** Google Maps — Teraz store locator (Phase 2)
